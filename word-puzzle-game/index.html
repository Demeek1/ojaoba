🔤 One-Letter Word Duel

Two players. Change one letter. Beat the clock.

How to play:
• You're given a word (e.g. BOOK).
• Change exactly one letter to make a new real word — same length (BOOK → TOOK).
• Then the other player continues from your word.
• You start with 10 seconds. The clock shrinks by 0.5s each turn, down to a 5-second floor.
• No repeating a word already used. First player to run out of time loses.
